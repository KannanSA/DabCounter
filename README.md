# DabCounter
Apple Watch app with CoreMotion
Privacy Policy
Effective Date: [19/03/2025]

Dab Counter ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data and protect your information when using our Apple Watch app.

1. Information We Collect
The App does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. The only data processed is motion sensor data (via CoreMotion) to detect dab gestures. This data is used locally on your device and is not transmitted, stored, or shared with third parties.

2. How We Use Motion Data
The motion data is used exclusively for detecting dabs and counting them within the App. We do not collect, store, or analyze any motion data beyond what is needed for functionality.

3. No Third-Party Sharing
We do not share any data with third parties. The App operates entirely offline and does not transmit user data to external servers.

4. Data Security
Since the App does not collect or store personal data, there are no security risks related to data transmission. All motion processing occurs directly on your Apple Watch.

5. Children’s Privacy
The App does not collect personal data and is safe for users of all ages. However, it is not specifically designed for children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy if necessary. Any changes will be reflected in the App or on the official App Store listing.
